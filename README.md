A local space for prep. AI is your trainer. 

## Rooms

- **AI Assisted Coding Challenges**: build and solve problems with an AI pair.
- **System Design Practice**: sketch and defend designs on a whiteboard.

## Stack

Tauri (Rust), Three.js, Excalidraw, local Whisper, and a Rust MCP server.

## Privacy

Reports, transcripts, and recordings stay on your machine, outside this repo.
